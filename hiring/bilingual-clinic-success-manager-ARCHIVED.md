## Why This Role Exists

Dentago is a B2B dental procurement marketplace launching in the UK, with France and Switzerland as the first European expansion markets (Days 301–315). French dental clinics require localised onboarding support — language barriers in procurement are a real friction point. This role bridges the gap between product and French-speaking clinic users.

---

## Responsibilities

- Onboard new French-speaking dental clinics to the Dentago platform
- Handle inbound support queries in English and French
- Run product demos for prospective clinics in France/Switzerland/Belgium
- Collect feedback and translate it to product/ops improvements
- Monitor clinic activation health and drive repeat usage
- Coordinate with UK success team on shared processes

---

## Requirements

- Fluent in English and French (native or near-native in both)
- Background in dental, healthcare, or B2B SaaS customer success preferred
- Strong communication skills — phone, email, video
- Comfortable with CRM and support tools
- Proactive, self-directed — this is an early-stage role

---

## Nice to Have

- Knowledge of French dental procurement landscape (Gacd, Acteon, etc.)
- Experience in a marketplace or procurement platform
- Swiss French or Belgian French familiarity
