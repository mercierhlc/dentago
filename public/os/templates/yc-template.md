## Personalisation rules

The `[specific thing]` line should be drawn from real signals in the lead data:

- **Review score ≥ 4.5**: "I noticed [company] has a strong [X]-star Google rating — clearly a practice that takes patient experience seriously"
- **Instagram/Facebook active**: "I saw [company]'s content — the team clearly puts real effort into the patient side of things"
- **Company description available**: summarise one specific fact from it
- **Multi-site / group**: "I saw [company] is running [N] sites — procurement gets more painful at that scale, not less"
- **Role = Finance/Director/CEO**: lean on spend visibility angle
- **Role = Clinical/Principal**: lean on time-saving / removing admin angle
- **Fallback**: "I came across [company] while mapping UK practices — your role as [job title] puts you right in the middle of the procurement headache"
