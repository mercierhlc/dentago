Common questions

What you ask before signing up.

Is Marketplace really free, or is there a catch?
It's free, forever, with no card required. We earn a small kickback from suppliers when you buy — never from you, and never on top of the price you see. Your account prices stay yours.
Do I keep my existing supplier accounts and pricing?
Yes. Dentago authenticates against your existing supplier accounts and pulls your real prices — including any negotiated deals or PO pricing. We don't replace your relationships, we surface them.
How long does Pro take to set up?
Most clinics are live in under an hour. Connect supplier accounts (4 min), import current stock from a CSV or our app (15 min), set par levels (auto-suggested), invite your team. We'll do it with you on a 30-min call if you'd like.
What does Pro cost for a multi-site group?
£299/practice/month, with volume discounts at 5+ sites and group-level analytics included. We work with DSOs and corporates on enterprise terms — book a demo.
Can I cancel Pro any time?
Yes — monthly rolling, cancel from your billing page. Your data stays yours and Marketplace continues to work for free. We'll export everything if you want to leave.
Is patient or treatment data ever exposed to suppliers?
No. Suppliers only see purchase orders. PMS-derived treatment volume is used internally to predict stock burn and never leaves your tenant. Dentago is GDPR & ISO 27001 aligned.
